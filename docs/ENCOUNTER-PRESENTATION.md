# Encounter presentation and achievement identity

Each outcome leaf owns its AI-authored resolution. Nested choices and challenge branches must supply complete narration; gifts supply a distinct name and description per award. Possessions are tangible objects; statuses are specific conditions or affiliations. No arrival or generic gift fallback may pass validation for new generations. Death includes its cause and finality.

Achievement IDs are scoped to world, cell and outcome; they do not include visit or life. Legacy identical achievements from the same cell are deduplicated. Award claims persist on the character: permanent awards once per source, other lifetimes once per source per life. Choices and their narrative consequences can recur according to their visit/life policy.

Peeks describe biome and visible infrastructure only, not population or occupancy. Occupied scenes retain population. Teleport manifestations belong in scene text and image input; option labels never disclose destination or consequences. Fatal teleport narration receives destination biome fields and actual blockers before resolution, without generating a destination image or an explorable cell.

Reward and death reveals are dismissible centered dialogs with icons and individual award details. Mandatory choices and teleport confirmation remain separate. Existing cells receive a cached prose-only upgrade as revisited; existing images are preserved and require explicit Dev regeneration. No world, account, seed or history reset accompanies this change.
