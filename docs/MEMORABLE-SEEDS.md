Seed: `the-sanctuary-is-run-by-otters`.

Sealed-origin rule: if and only if all four physical exits are blocked, create four direction-specific deterministic teleport exits. Adjacent terrain remains blocked. Destinations obey ordinary teleport rules, including potentially fatal arrivals; the origin remains safe. Telegraph and illustrate the portals without exposing their destinations. Dev travel remains isolated.

Give up is always available to living characters, including during pending options or teleports. It ends the current life, clears pending interactions, applies ordinary temporary-trait loss, and preserves standings, badges and lifetime progress. Return to origin begins a fresh life. No repeat surrender awards.
